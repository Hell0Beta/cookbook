1

In a small bowl, whisk together the lemon juice, lemon zest, olive oil, dried oregano, salt, and black pepper to create the marinade.

2

Place the chicken breasts in a shallow dish or a resealable plastic bag. Pour the marinade over the chicken, ensuring it is evenly coated. Let it marinate in the refrigerator for at least .

3

Preheat the grill to medium-high heat. Grill the chicken breasts until they are cooked through and have nice grill marks, about 6 minutes per side.
